Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does the wrapping take?

Most orders are ready in 1–2 days. For urgent or same-day requests, message us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Do you provide the gift, or only wrap it?

We focus on wrapping — you provide the gift and we make it look stunning. If you'd like help sourcing a gift or hamper items, just ask and we'll guide you.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. We offer home collection and pickup across Kota. Share your location on WhatsApp and we'll arrange the most convenient option for you.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the gift size, wrapping style, and materials chosen. Send us a quick message on WhatsApp with your details and we'll share a quote right away.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Absolutely. We regularly handle bulk wedding, festival, and corporate orders, with volume discounts available. Reach out early so we can schedule larger batches smoothly.

How do I place an order?

Simply send an enquiry through the form below, message us on WhatsApp at +91 95716 28921, or give us a call. We'll take it from there.

What are the payment options?

You can pay on delivery via UPI or cash. For bulk and corporate orders, we'll confirm the payment terms when you book.

Can I choose a specific theme or colour palette?

Absolutely. Share your occasion, colours, or a reference picture and we'll match the wrapping to your theme — from soft pastels to rich festive and metallic palettes.

Do you wrap odd-sized, bulky, or delicate items?

Yes. We handle everything from small favours to large hampers, outfits, and fragile pieces — using the right materials and padding so each gift is protected and presented beautifully.

Do you add personalised tags or message cards?

We do. Custom name tags, message cards, and small finishing touches can be added on request to make your gift feel truly personal.

How far in advance should I book?

For single gifts, 1–2 days is usually enough. For weddings, festivals, and bulk orders we recommend booking a week or more ahead so we can plan larger batches comfortably.

Which areas do you serve in Kota?

We offer home collection and pickup across Kota. Share your location on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the most convenient collection and delivery option for you.
